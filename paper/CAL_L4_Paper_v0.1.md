## Abstract

The Cognitive Abstraction Layer (CAL) architecture proposes that scalable AI governance requires not larger context windows, but semantically conservative hierarchical compression. Layer 4 (L4) — this paper — addresses the top of this hierarchy: meta-inference over compressed tensor volumes V without human working memory as a substrate.

We formalize the **L4 Efficiency Hypothesis**: there exists an inference architecture such that the cost of M(V) scales with κ(V) — the structural complexity of V — where κ(V) grows significantly more slowly than O(n²) in the number of raw artifacts n. This decouples inference cost from artifact volume and couples it to governance state complexity.

We introduce the **Representational Convergence Conjecture (RCC)**: that the representation optimal for human governance (CAL's V at high SID) and the representation optimal for the generator's own computation converge — or are related by a tractable translation. If true, a single compressed state would simultaneously serve human oversight and generator compute-efficiency: the *north star* of the architecture.

This paper reports the framework and its now-met preconditions. Three conditions must hold before the L4 Efficiency Hypothesis can be empirically tested: (a) the composition operator C (L3) must be defined and validated, (b) the O(n²) flat-context baseline must be empirically measured on hardware, and (c) governance accuracy under both approaches must be quantified. **As of June 2026, (a) and (b) are met:** L3 closed with C = C_causal ∘ C_compress delivering κ(V)=1296 (195.6× compression) at causal conservation U≈0.86, and AMD-Instinct measured the flat-context curve at n^1.90–1.91 (R²≈0.997, CONFIRMED quadratic across two independent runs). With both met, the **κ vs n² cost contrast was executed on MI300X**: the flat-context cost grows as a power law in artifact volume (≈n^1.91) while the governance-state inference cost is bounded by κ(V) and **independent of n** — the decoupling ratio grows to 52.8× across seqLen 512→4096. This demonstrates the **mechanism** of the L4 Efficiency Hypothesis (cost coupling vs decoupling), not a production-scale or accuracy claim. Condition (c) — governance accuracy of M(V) vs flat-context — is the remaining gate (RCT-adjacent).

**Keywords:** meta-inference, efficiency hypothesis, semantic compression, governance inference, tensor volume, representational convergence, AMD-Instinct, flash attention

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The Inference Cost Problem

Current transformer architectures pay an attention cost that scales with O(n²) in context length n. For AI governance over multi-agent pipelines, n is the number of raw artifacts — code files, logs, configs — generated by concurrent agents. As agent pipelines scale, this cost becomes a structural barrier: not a hardware constraint, but an architectural one. Flat-context inference over L0 artifacts re-evaluates every pairwise relationship at every forward pass, including relationships that are semantically redundant given the pipeline's causal structure.

The CAL hypothesis — instantiated at L2 by TCO-L2 [Chancay 2026] — is that semantic compression preserves the governance-relevant structure while eliminating this redundancy. L3 [Chancay 2026b] defines the composition operator C that produces the compressed volume V. L4 defines what M(V) can do with V — and at what cost.

### 1.2 Position in the CAL Architecture

```
L4  Meta-Inference         M(V) → {decisions, predictions, adaptations}    ← THIS PAPER
     ↑ gate-C: requires V from L3
L3  Tensor Volume          V = C({T⁽ˢ⁾}_{s=1}^{n})                         ← [Chancay 2026b]
     ↑ aggregates T from L2 sessions
L2  Cognitive Tensor       T[d,i,j,k] + NCF                                  ← TCO-L2 [Chancay 2026]
L1  Semantic Features      quality vectors V ∈ [0,1]¹¹
L0  Raw Artifacts          AI-generated code, configs, logs
```

L4 is the fully automated inference layer. No human working memory is required as the inference substrate — but this autonomy is conditional: M(V) is only coherent if C (L3) is semantically conservative. If C fails to preserve causal structure, M(V) degrades silently.

## 2. Formal Definition

```
M: V → {decisions, predictions, adaptations}
```

The meta-inference function M operates on the tensor volume V produced by the L3 composition operator C. Because V (by the properties required of C) encodes causal relationships, temporal coherence, and dimensional stability, M can perform reasoning that is structurally impossible at lower layers:

| Capability | Description | L2 analog |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Causal discovery** | Identify which quality dimensions causally influence others, across sessions | Human reads Ρ — inter-agent conflicts |
| **Drift prediction** | Predict future quality trajectories from historical volume dynamics, before threshold crossing | Human reads Δ — trend signal |
| **Cross-pipeline conflict detection** | Identify conflicts spanning multiple sessions or pipelines | Human reads Ρ across time |
| **Policy generation** | Produce governance policies from historical patterns; pass to L2 for human review or direct injection | Human produces P_new at NCF |

The key distinction: L2 human orchestration requires the human to actively read {Ω, Δ, Ρ, Ξ} and inject policy. M(V) performs the equivalent inference automatically — without the human as the working-memory substrate, but accountable to the human at L2 for high-stakes decisions.

## 3. The L4 Efficiency Hypothesis

### 3.1 Formal Statement

> **L4 Efficiency Hypothesis (§6.2 CAL pre-paper):** there exists an inference architecture such that the cost of M(V) scales with κ(V) — the structural complexity of V (e.g., its effective rank, the entropy of its attractor distribution, the size of its causal graph) — where κ(V) grows **significantly more slowly than O(n²)** in n (the raw artifact count of the underlying L0 space).

### 3.2 What This Claims and What It Does Not

This is **not** a claim that meta-inference is exponentially cheaper than attention in all cases. It is a claim that:

> if semantic abstraction is semantically conservative (i.e., SID(Lk→Lk+1) > θ at each boundary), then inference cost can be decoupled from the volume of the original artifact stream and coupled instead to the structural complexity of the compressed governance state.

The claim is conditional on the success of L3. If C fails to compress V while preserving causal structure — if SID(L2→L3) < θ — then M(V) is inferring over a corrupted representation and the efficiency claim is moot.

### 3.3 Required Conditions

| Condition | Description | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **(a)** C defined + κ(V) concrete | L3 composition operator validated on synthetic corpus; effective rank κ(V) measured | **MET (2026-06) — L3 closed.** C = C_causal ∘ C_compress (dual Architecture A); κ(V)=1296 (195.6× compression), causal conservation U≈0.86 (75% of headroom recovered). See §3.5. |
| **(b)** O(n²) baseline measured empirically | Flat-context attention cost on real hardware (MI300X) for seqLen 512→4k | **MET (2026-06) — AMD-Instinct.** `fa_robust` seqLen sweep: exponent n^1.90, R²=0.996, CONFIRMED quadratic. |
| **(c)** Governance accuracy under both approaches | Accuracy of M(V) vs. flat-context LLM on governance decisions; L2 corpus provides the reference | Pending — gate (a)+(b) now met; this is the remaining gate (RCT-adjacent). |

With (a) and (b) met, the **κ vs n² cost contrast** (the hypothesis's quantitative core) was **executed on MI300X** (§5.4): L4-A delivers the (κ, G_pruned, U) object, and the contrast confirms the cost-law decoupling that the hypothesis predicts (mechanism level). Condition (c) — that M(V) decisions match or beat flat-context — is the remaining gate and is RCT-adjacent (Paper 2). The mechanism contrast does **not** substitute for (c): it shows the cost shape, not that the cheaper inference is as accurate.

**Pre-registration required before testing the hypothesis.** Commit the exact statistical test and acceptance criteria before running any experiment that bears on condition (c) or the at-scale cost contrast.

### 3.4 κ(V) as Structural Complexity

κ(V) is the key operationalization. Three candidate definitions, ordered by tractability:

1. **Effective rank of Tucker core G** — most tractable; directly from L3's Tucker decomposition. κ(V) = rank(G). **Adopted:** L3's S2 confirmed κ(V)=1296 at rank (8,3,3,3,6), a 195.6× compression of the session tensor, validated tractable (sub-linear in n_sessions).
2. **Attractor entropy** — entropy of the distribution over attractor states in V; requires identifying attractors first.
3. **Causal graph size** — number of edges in the recovered causal graph; complementary to candidate 1 in L4-A's dual representation (|E|=2 after pruning), and the object the human governs with.

Candidate 1 is the operative κ(V) for the cost contrast; candidate 3 is the governance object carried alongside it (see §3.5).

### 3.5 L3 Result Summary — what C delivers to L4 (2026-06)

L3 closed (characterization phase, ~95% per the methodological review). The path: Tucker preserves variance but not causal structure (S3-bis: reconstruction F1=0.135); a ground-truth-free causal metric U was validated (TCI); causality was shown to be **structural, not magnitude** (proxy audit); Tucker's failure mode is **spurious-edge fabrication, not loss** (Q_L3.2A: coverage and sign held, reachability/|E| exploded); and a **structural prune** to the raw causal support recovers 75% of the causal-conservation headroom **with no ground truth** (Form 1: U 0.441→0.862, raw↔GT gap = 0.000).

The operator C delivered to L4 is **Architecture A (dual representation)**:

```
C: T → V = (V_Tucker, G_pruned)
   V_Tucker : Tucker core, κ(V)=1296, 195.6× compression  → the cost object (O(κ) side)
   G_pruned : causal graph pruned to the raw support, |E|=2 → the governance object
```

A reference meta-inference `M_ref` was shown to operate on this dual pair at (κ+|E|) cost **without re-running causal discovery** — the prune is *carried*, not *re-derived* (L4-A checks C1/C2/C5 all pass). The honest limitation, declared: **κ does not reflect the prune** — κ is the Tucker core's; the causal cleanup lives in the second object. Collapsing the two into a single volume V′ (so κ itself reflects causal sparsity) is **L4-B**, a research hypothesis requiring an inverse graph→tensor projection, gated on first characterizing the residual 25% (where U falls short of 1.0). L4-A is the operational baseline; L4-B is future work.

## 4. The Representational Convergence Conjecture (RCC)

### 4.1 Statement

> **Representational Convergence Conjecture (§6.4 CAL pre-paper):** the representation that is *governance-optimal* (minimal sufficient for human-supervisable causal observability — CAL's V at high SID) and the representation that is *compute-optimal* (minimal sufficient internal state for the generator to continue producing semantically coherent output without re-deriving full context at each step) converge to the same object — or to two objects related by a tractable, possibly learned, translation.

### 4.2 Motivation

Current transformer inference pays O(n²) cost for context of *any* semantic structure — a 100k-token context describing a simple state costs the same as one describing a complex one. The L4 Efficiency Hypothesis (§3) observes that inference cost should couple to κ(V), not n. The RCC pushes one step further: if V persists as a compressed semantic substrate, a generator *coupled to V* would not re-generate semantic density from scratch at each step — it would emit *deltas* against the persistent state and let an update operator fold them in.

Under the RCC, the substrate doing this folding is *the same substrate CAL maintains for human governance*. The same V that a human reads at L2 NCF would be the V that the generator updates and reads during inference.

### 4.3 Why This Is Kept Separate from L2

L2 controls *pipeline transformation* — what the agentic system produces — via natural-language re-orchestration. The RCC concerns *compute-side transformability*: whether the generator can be reorganized to exploit the same compressed state.

These are different planes. L2 is governance over pipeline outputs. The RCC is a hypothesis about generator internals. **The CHI 2027 paper (TCO-L2) makes no RCC claims.** The RCC is L4-level speculation.

### 4.4 The Hard Requirement

The conjecture's difficulty is concentrated here: CAL's φ is today a *non-differentiable, human-legible* compressor (static analysis + discrete LLM-judge). For the RCC to hold, there must exist a φ′ that is simultaneously:
- Legible to a human supervisor (as φ currently is)
- Couplable to the generator's gradient or latent dynamics

Whether such φ′ exists — and whether the human-optimal and compute-optimal compressions actually coincide rather than merely correlate — is open. The negation is entirely plausible.

### 4.5 Relation to AMD-Instinct

`probe_mfma_mapping.hip` in AMD-Instinct already characterized the lane↔output mapping of `v_mfma_f32_16x16x16f16` — the low-level register access that coupling a generator to V at inference time would require. With C now defined at L3 (2026-06), this makes the RCC empirically approachable from the hardware side.

### 4.6 Falsifiability

The RCC predicts:
- **(a)** For a generator coupled to persistent semantic state V, inference cost decouples from raw context length and couples to κ(V) (the L4 Efficiency Hypothesis as special case).
- **(b)** A single learned translation suffices to map between governance state V and generator update state, with bounded reconstruction loss on both governance accuracy *and* generation quality.

Failure of (b) — where minimizing governance-SID and minimizing generation loss require provably distinct representations — refutes the strong form.

### 5.4 The κ vs n² Cost Contrast — Result (2026-06)

With C delivered by L3 (§3.5) and the O(n²) baseline measured (§5.2), the cost
contrast was run on MI300X (gfx942, ROCm 7.2), pre-registered as S5
(`PRE_REGISTRATION_S5_COST_CONTRAST.md`). It combines the two cost laws:

```
O(n²) side (flat context)   : Cost_flat(n) = 8.07e-4 · n^1.907   (R²=0.9967, D=128, MFMA)
O(κ) side (governance state): Cost_gov     = κ(V_Tucker) + |E| ≈ 1322   (independent of n)
```

The reported quantity is the **decoupling ratio** D(n) = Cost_flat(n)/Cost_gov,
normalized to n=512:

| seqLen n | Cost_flat | Cost_gov | D(n) |
|----------|-----------|----------|------|
| 512  | 118.6 µs | 1322 (const) | 1.00× |
| 1024 | 444.8 µs | 1322 (const) | 3.75× |
| 2048 | 1668.3 µs | 1322 (const) | 14.07× |
| 4096 | 6257.6 µs | 1322 (const) | **52.77×** |

`Cost_flat` are values of the **fitted law** `c·n^p` (the column from which D(n) is
computed in `s5_cost_contrast.py`), not the raw measured per-seqLen latencies — those
appear in the baseline note (n^1.90 sweep, e.g. 103.7 / 6015.1 µs at n=512 / 4096) and
agree with the fit within measurement noise. D(n) is the dimensionless ratio of the two
laws, so it is invariant to this distinction.

D(n) grows monotonically with a fitted exponent of 1.907 (R²=1.0) — i.e. the gap
widens with artifact volume exactly as the flat-context law grows, while the
governance-state cost stays flat. **Mechanism confirmed:** flat-context inference
cost is coupled to n; governance-state inference cost is bounded by κ(V) and
decoupled from n.

**Three honesty constraints, stated (inherited from L4-A's declared limitations):**

1. **Different `n`.** κ(V)=1296 is at the governance-corpus scale (n=30 sessions,
   11 dimensions), **not** the seqLen scale of the baseline (512→4096 tokens). S5
   does not plug κ and seqLen onto one axis to claim a single speedup number; it
   contrasts the two cost **laws** (coupling vs decoupling). The decoupling ratio is
   a dimensionless shape comparison, not a wall-clock speedup at a fixed n.
2. **Mechanism, not production scale.** This shows the cost *shape* the hypothesis
   predicts; it does not establish that κ(V)=1296 holds at production governance
   scale, which needs a larger L2 corpus.
3. **κ does not reflect the prune.** κ is the Tucker core's, fixed before causal
   pruning; the causal cleanup lives in G_pruned (the dual representation, §3.5).

The optional hardware probe of the O(κ) side (giving it a wall-clock latency
comparable to the O(n²) side) was **omitted** per the pre-registration: the
n-independent read-count is already the correct cost object, and a new MFMA kernel
for a 1296-element core was not justified. It is noted as future work.

**Freeze.** Per the methodological review, S5 results were frozen before the residual
characterization (§5.5) opened: this prevented getting ahead of L4-B before knowing
what the residual contains. With the freeze held, that characterization (L4-B0) then
ran — and decided against L4-B (§5.5).

### 5.5 Residual Characterization (L4-B0) — and why L4-B is not opened

With S5 frozen, we pre-registered (`PRE_REGISTRATION_L4B0_RESIDUAL.md`) and ran a
**descriptive** characterization of the residual `ΔU = 1 − U(Φ_masked) ≈ 0.138` that
the Form-1 prune did not recover. The question is the prior for L4-B: of that residual,
what fraction is **linear-edge-representable** (structure a linear inverse projection
could fold into a single V′) versus not carriable by a single linear V′? Because U is
the Pearson correlation of the off-diagonal entries of the continuous **signed** flow
matrix Φ (ParCorr val_matrix, lag 1), the attribution is performed in Φ-space, not on
edge sets. Two buckets are linear-edge-representable — **B1** (on-support magnitude
error, same sign) and **B2** (sign flip) — and their complement **B3-lin** plus any
**B4** (lag>1) structure is not. We attribute ΔU by correcting each bucket's entries to
their raw Φ value and recomputing U, then test `share_lin = (ΔU_{B1}+ΔU_{B2})/ΔU`
against a pre-committed GO≥0.70 / CONDITIONAL / NO-GO<0.40 rule.

| Bucket | ΔU (mean G1/G2/G3) | share of ΔU | linear-V′-carriable |
|--------|-----|-----|-----|
| B1 — magnitude | +0.027 | 19.3% | yes |
| B2 — sign flip | +0.000 | 0.0% | yes |
| **B3-lin — complement** | **+0.111** | **80.7%** | **no (non-linearity)** |
| B4 — lag>1 | — | 0.0% of support | n/a (absent) |

The result is **`share_lin = 0.193` → NO-GO** (reproducibility check D1 passed; the ΔU
budget closes exactly). Only 19.3% of the residual is linear-edge-representable; 80.7%
lives in B3-lin. B4 = 0% confirms the residual is **not** higher-order temporal (the
S1-bis corpus is lag-1 by construction), so what a single linear V′ cannot carry is
**non-linearity**, not lag structure. Folding the prune into a single linear volume
therefore cannot rescue the causal information the dual carries in `G_pruned`.

**Consequence for the architecture.** The dual representation `V = (V_Tucker, G_pruned)`
is the **terminal** form at this rank: L4-B (a single-V′ via inverse graph→tensor
projection) is **not opened**, because its premise — that the residual is linear
structure an inverse projection can absorb — is refuted. This bounds the RCC (§4): a
*single linear* compressed state cannot simultaneously be κ-minimal and causally
complete here; the convergence the RCC conjectures, if it exists, is not a low-rank
linear folding at this scale. We report this as a **negative result** — the second time
in the C programme that the ordering *causality ≻ reconstruction* prevents a collapse
(after the S3-bis refutation of low-rank Tucker as a causality-preserving C).

A direct non-linear measurement of B3 (GPDC/CMIknn) was not run: both were infeasible
in the frozen analysis environment, and — since L4-B's V′ is linear — a non-linear test
is redundant with the linear complement (whatever the linear machinery cannot recover is
exactly what a linear V′ could not carry). No environment mutation was introduced, so the
ParCorr machinery that validated L3 stays frozen.

## 8. SID at L4

SID(L3→L4) measures how much decision-relevant information is preserved through M. The measurement challenge at L4 is the same as at L3 — no human reference signal — but compounded: M operates on V rather than on T, and the governance decisions it produces span longer time horizons.

Three candidate measurement approaches (§7.3 CAL pre-paper):

1. **Downstream outcome tracking:** measure whether M(V) governance decisions lead to measurable pipeline improvements over time. Requires longitudinal deployment.
2. **Synthetic benchmarks with known causal structure:** M(V) decisions on synthetic pipelines with ground-truth causal graphs. Directly reuses L3's S3 infrastructure.
3. **L2 human comparison on held-out cases:** compare M(V) decisions against expert human L2 decisions on novel scenarios not seen during training.

Method 2 is the starting point: it is immediately composable with L3's synthetic corpus and does not require deployment.

## 9. Relationship to Existing Work

- **Flash Attention** [Dao et al. 2022]: IO-aware implementation of O(n²) attention — the baseline Rol 1 measures. `fa_dme` extends with DME async on MI300X.
- **State Space Models (Mamba/S4)** [Gu & Dao 2023]: demonstrate sub-quadratic temporal inference with persistent compressed state — structural analog of M(V) operating on V.
- **Mixture of Experts (MoE)**: token routing as a form of sparse inference — κ(V) as effective rank is related to the number of active experts needed for governance decisions.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: V as a structured, semantically compressed knowledge base for governance — M as a specialized retriever/reasoner over V.
- **Tucker/CP Decomposition** [Kolda & Bader 2009]: L3's primary C candidate; defines κ(V) for L4.
- **Causal Representation Learning** [Schölkopf et al. 2021]: M must perform causal discovery over V — the same framework that constrains C at L3.
